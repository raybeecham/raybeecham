Engineering principles

The guardrails are part of the product.

Trustworthy technical systems are not defined only by what they can calculate. They are also defined by what they refuse to claim, which inputs they reject, how they record uncertainty, and where they stop.

01

Evidence before claims

Keep provenance, limitations, contradictions, and unresolved unknowns visible.

02

Mission before technology

Begin with the decision context and success criteria, not a preferred platform or vendor.

03

Baselines before advantage

Compare experimental methods against credible classical alternatives under shared conditions.

04

Reproducibility by design

Version configurations, preserve seeds and environment metadata, test contracts, and emit machine-readable artifacts.

05

Human accountability

Decision support can inform a responsible owner. It cannot authorize procurement, deployment, clinical action, or operational control.

06

Security boundaries matter

Protect credentials, constrain targets, fail closed, and distinguish lab evidence from production assurance.
