Engineering principles

The guardrails are part of the system.

Trustworthy engineering is defined not only by what a platform can calculate, but also by what it refuses to infer, what evidence it rejects, how it preserves uncertainty, and where it stops.

01

Evidence before claims

Provenance, limitations, contradictions, and unresolved unknowns stay visible.

02

Mission before technology

Start with the decision context and success criteria, not a preferred platform or vendor.

03

Baselines before advantage

Compare experimental methods against credible classical alternatives under shared conditions.

04

Reproducibility by design

Version configurations, preserve metadata, test contracts, and emit machine-readable artifacts.

05

Human accountability

Decision support informs a responsible owner. It does not inherit authorization.

06

Security boundaries matter

Protect credentials, constrain targets, fail closed, and distinguish lab evidence from production assurance.
